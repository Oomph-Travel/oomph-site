Why this sailing

Mid-July is when Southeast Alaska is at full strength — long daylight, salmon running, whales feeding in the Inside Passage.

This departure threads Seward to Vancouver in seven days on a ship that carries a few hundred guests, not a few thousand — which is what lets it call on Haines at all. The shore event on July 19 is the reason I'm featuring this sailing: a small-group wildlife excursion that ends with a salmon charcuterie spread on the beach, arranged exclusively for guests booked through the Distinctive Voyages program. You will not find it in the ship's excursion catalog.

If Alaska has been on your list and you want the version with fewer people and better food, this is the week I'd point you to.

Questions I get asked

Before you ask

Does booking through you cost more?

No. The cruise fare is set by Silversea and is the same whether you book direct or with me. The host, reception, and shore event are added at no extra cost through my network membership.

Is this a group cruise? Will I be locked into group activities?

No. You sail as an independent guest with full run of the ship. The welcome reception and shore event are invitations, not obligations.

What if the sailing sells out or the program fills?

Amenity space on these departures is limited and separate from the ship's overall capacity. If the program fills, I'll tell you straight and we'll look at the next departure that carries the same inclusions.

Can you also handle flights, hotels, and the days around the cruise?

Yes — pre- and post-cruise planning is most of what I do. Anchorage or Vancouver time on either end of this sailing is worth building in, and I'll map that with you.
