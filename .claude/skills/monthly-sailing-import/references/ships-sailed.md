## How to ask

Keep it short and scoped to the batch. Something like:

> This month's batch includes Silver Muse, Silver Moon, Queen Mary 2, and EXPLORA III —
> none of which are on your sailed list. Any of those you've actually been aboard?

Then record the answer here, including any detail he offers. A remembered specific — a deck,
a meal, a port morning — is worth far more in a blurb than the bare fact of having sailed,
so capture it verbatim when it comes.
